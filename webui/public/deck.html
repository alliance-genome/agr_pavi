The pipeline, precisely

DNA in, aligned protein out

Retrievepysam · faidx
FastaFile.fetch(seq_id, start, end) on the bgzip/faidx reference assembly; exon & CDS regions spliced, −strand reverse-complemented.
TranslateBiopython
CDS assembled frame-aware (or longest ORF across 3 frames), then Seq.translate(Standard, to_stop=True).
Variantson coding DNA
HGVS variants applied to the coding sequence and re-translated; ORF re-validated; non-coding dropped by SO consequence.
Align & mapClustal Omega
clustalo --outfmt=clu (or MAFFT L-INS-i); each residue index projected onto its gapped alignment column.

Getting the most out of it

Four things worth knowing

›

Load Example gives you a working multi-species job in one click.

›

Paste a full HGVS — NC_000068.8:g.105521966G>T — to pull in a specific variant.

›

Big genes load ~the first 100 variants; find the rest by HGVS or allele name.

›

My Jobs keeps your history; Edit reopens any past query.
